Adaptive travel and health questionnaire

Itemised questions that adapt as you answer. They identify the treatment and care the trip has to carry, capture what you need from where you stay, and filter activities down to the ones that actually suit you.

Your answers stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to a server, and the page is blocked from making network requests. You choose what to export at the end.

Step 1

    This questionnaire is a planning tool. It is not clinical advice, it does not assess whether you are fit to travel, and it does not replace your clinician or your insurer. Take the brief to both.
