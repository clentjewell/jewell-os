# Familiar Spirits — project plan v01 (Draft)

Baseline: Asana task [Familiar Spirits](https://app.asana.com/1/1172125425202894/project/1209934477999663/task/1217222265320586),
created 6 August 2026 by Ronnie. It sits in Big Rocks on Clent - 2026 and in New Tasks on
Jewell Team - Overview 2025. It carries 4 subtasks and nothing else: no notes, no client
field, no assignees, no dates.

This plan turns that stub into a runnable engagement plan on the `3D Process`. Once the
client folder is scaffolded in Drive, the canonical home for this document is
`Familiar Spirits / 00 Admin / 05 Timelines + Project Control`. This repo copy is the
working draft for review.

## 1. What the baseline tells us

The 4 subtasks, in dependency order:

1. **Justin - provide more information** — the engagement is blocked on client-side input.
2. **Reverse brief** — we play back our understanding before committing to anything.
3. **Scope** — tier, price and boundaries agreed. The board's "Quote Needed" status suggests
   a quote is expected.
4. **Discover** — Phase 1 of the 3D Process begins only after the first 3 close.

What the baseline does not tell us: who Familiar Spirits are, what they sell, who Justin is
to the business, the budget, or the deadline. The repo holds no prior record of Familiar
Spirits or Justin. A shelved "Boozebakery" spirits concept exists on the incubator shelf;
no link is established and none is assumed. Everything in section 4 onward is therefore
indicative until Stage 0 closes.

## 2. Objective

Take Familiar Spirits from an empty Asana stub to a signed, scoped engagement running on the
3D Process, with the standard Drive shell, Asana project and artefact registry in place, and
Gate 1 (Discovery readout) delivered.

## 4. Stage plan

### Stage 0 — Information (maps to "Justin - provide more information")

Blocked on Justin. Actions to unblock:

- Send Justin the CORE link and current access code (per the CORE intake runbook). The 4
  CORE questions — Customers, Offering, Rivals, Expression — take about 15 minutes and land
  automatically in the console.
- If CORE is too heavy for where the relationship is, a short call or voice note covering
  the same 4 headings is an acceptable substitute.
- Minimum information to exit Stage 0: what the business is, what Justin wants from us, why
  now, and any hard deadline or budget signal.

Exit: information received and filed in `06 Source + Working + Imports / 01 Raw Source Files`.

### Stage 1 — Reverse brief (maps to "Reverse brief")

- Draft a 1-page reverse brief from the Stage 0 material: our understanding of the business,
  the problem, the desired outcome, and what we would do about it.
- Clent reviews; send to Justin for a yes / no / not-quite.
- Exit: Justin confirms the reverse brief reflects his intent.

### Stage 2 — Scope and commercials (maps to "Scope")

- Pick the service tier and price from the reverse brief. Draft the quote.
- File under `00 Admin`: qualification note (06), deal memo (07), proposal and pricing (08).
- Decision point (Tier 3, Clent): proceed to proposal, park, or decline. "Do it" means
  approval; anything less is not approval.
- Exit: signed scope or SOW in `00 Admin / 04 Commercial + SOW`; close and kickoff noted
  in `00 Admin / 09 Close + Kickoff`.

### Stage 3 — Setup

- Scaffold the standard client shell: Drive folders (00 Admin through 99 Archive), Asana
  project, `#client-familiar-spirits` Slack channel, Artifact-Registry, KH00 stub.
- Confirm scaffolding questions from the core skill: client-specific output codes, long-form
  client name for filenames (proposed: Familiar-Spirits), and whether the engagement is
  scoped through Gate 2 only or through Deploy.
- Update Asana: set the Client field, assignees and due dates on all subtasks.

### Stage 4 — Discover (maps to "Discover")

Phase 1 of the 3D Process, standard artefact set:

| Code | Artefact |
| --- | --- |
| D01 | Discovery Questions |
| D02 | Audience Teardown |
| D03 | Competitor Analysis |
| D04 | Killer Questions or Offer Worksheet (confirm which before assigning) |
| D05 | Offer Worksheet or SWOT (confirm which before assigning) |
| D06 | Discovery Pack Synthesis |
| D07 | Discovery Readout |
| D08-DECK | Gate 1 Deck |

Exit: Gate 1 readout delivered; sign-off recorded in `00 Admin / 03 Approvals + Signoffs`.

### Beyond Gate 1 (indicative, not committed)

Design (DS01–DS20) to Gate 2, then Deploy (DD00 Launch Playbook onward), then the Deepen +
Intelligence rhythm (IB, KH00, quarterly reviews). Scope for these phases is set at Stage 2
and confirmed at Gate 1. Nothing here is promised to the client yet.

## 6. Risks and open questions

- **Single dependency.** The whole plan hangs on Justin. Mitigation: one chase at day 5,
  park at day 10.
- **Unknown fit.** Until Stage 0 we cannot tell whether this is a Jewell client engagement,
  a partner venture, or an incubator concept. The reverse brief settles this; if it turns
  out to be an own-venture, Stages 2–3 change shape (no quote; deal memo becomes a venture
  memo) and this plan gets revised.
- **Category compliance.** If Familiar Spirits is an alcohol brand, Australian alcohol
  advertising rules (ABAC) apply to all outward copy. Confirm at Stage 1 and record in the
  reverse brief.
- **Asana hygiene.** The task has no client value, owner or dates. Fix at Stage 3, or
  earlier if the board review needs it.
